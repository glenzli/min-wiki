## Learning and representation boundary

The question is how records locate an event or process in deep time, then how production and consumption can leave different oxygen outcomes. Time remains a labelled linear ruler with the full 4540 Ma reference visible in every enlarged window. It is deliberately 2D: a fictional globe animation would add unsupported geographic detail. The oxygen ledger is arbitrary-unit bookkeeping, and the rock slab is an original diagram rather than a sampled specimen.
