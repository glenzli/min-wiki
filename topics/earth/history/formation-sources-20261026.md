# Earth-history formation-to-present journey: evidence and display boundaries

Source checks: **2026-10-26**, using actual web search, open and find requests. This note covers only the new `journeyModel.ts` / `journeyContent.json` presentation projection. The existing 28 events, their dates, the linear geological ruler, the independent oxygen ledger and its ten saved keys are retained. Existing topic sources remain in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Learning and ownership

The central question is how a changing Earth is inferred from surviving records, rather than how to make an ancient globe look certain. Readers select a representative stage and compare a globe, a plausible local environment and enlarged material clues. The observable result is a different qualitative surface, process or evidence construction under the same selected age/event. The causal questions follow accretion and heating → material separation → possible Moon-forming impact → crust/water evidence → microbial records and oxygen cycling → ecological, climatic and extinction records → modern comparisons.

The principal hazards are treating interpolated land as paleogeography; a model phase as years; an illustrative cutaway as a core photograph; a water clue as life detection; a surviving life record as an origin date; or a wide ice-age band as one uninterrupted global ice sheet. Both languages qualify these distinctions beside the relevant view/stage. Core formation is distinct from later solid-inner-core formation. The mantle is not described as a global liquid ocean.

The pure projection owns representative selection, bounded qualitative cues and reversible sampling. The controller owns selected state, playback, lenses and lifecycle; scene modules own geometry, resources and cameras. No scientific state or clock is moved into Reader. The seven related topic IDs point to the existing interior, life, climate, rocks, minerals, ores and ocean studies rather than reproducing their full models.

## Temporal and visual contract

- `HISTORY_STATIONS` selects 12 existing event IDs and derives ages directly from `EVENTS`. Approximate event dates are not replaced with dates from a new source. The full ruler stays linear.
- Adjacent representative pairs occupy equal **display** time. The first 45% of each interval holds the older station’s exact age and event for observation; the remaining 55% makes an accelerated age transition toward the next station. This is a teaching traversal, not a geological rate. `progress` is a local display phase; `journeyFraction` is the whole display position. Neither is physical integration time, a probability or a measured process fraction.
- Differentiation (`separation`) and Moon formation (`moon`) remain distinct at the same retained approximate 4500 Ma marker. The interval between them inserts no geological time. Its transient cursor can preserve a display subphase, while the old saved state alone restores the appropriate canonical boundary.
- A third `journeyFrame` cursor argument is used only if sampling it reproduces the same normalized 0.001 Ma age and exact event identity. Otherwise the frame derives its canonical position from the old state. A stale cursor cannot overwrite science state.
- `eventId` may be any of the existing 28 clues; `stationId` identifies the older representative interval anchor. Intermediate frames are **qualitative display transitions**, not dated reconstructions of every intervening event. The UI must distinguish representative context from the selected-event explanation. In particular, an interpolated impact/haze cue cannot be described as an impact or aftermath physically persisting throughout the 66 Ma-to-present interval.
- Core, melt, ocean, land, ice, haze, vegetation and impact values are independent authored drawing intensities in `[0,1]`. They are not measured area percentages, atmospheric concentrations, global curves or a conservation law. Profiles remain fixed throughout the observation hold, while local process geometry can advance from the same display phase; only the remaining transition uses smooth display easing. Matching profiles around the equal-age pair avoid numerical jumps without asserting that the two processes were one event.
- Ancient land shapes have no assigned geographical coordinates or reconstructed continent identities. A local example is not a measured landing site on that globe. Without a verified geographical asset, even modern procedural land is labelled illustrative.
- Material/micro views are enlarged teaching constructions, **not sample photographs or scans**. Different families use different scales. Mineral growth colors are not measured isotope values; particles are not diagnostic shock-deformation evidence; generic fossil impressions are not identified specimens or ecological recovery counts.
