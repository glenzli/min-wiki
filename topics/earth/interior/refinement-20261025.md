# Interior scene refinement, 2026-10-25

The question remains how evidence constrains Earth's interior and how different plate-boundary prerequisites produce different outcomes. Composition/depth, wave/station, boundary/motion, reversible geological progress and Rift A conditions stay topic-owned. The main plate section now retains closed crust/lithospheric-mantle volumes, old plate identities and material coordinates. Connected ridge fill, slab bending, crustal shortening/root thickening and horizontal channel offset consume `plateFrame`'s effective progress; the no-relative-motion comparison retains the selected coordinate and shows the initial geometry.

Two presentation state fields are added compatibly: `interiorWaveProgress` (integer 0–100, default fully revealed 100) and `interiorPlateCamera` (bounded finite vector JSON, independent of the true-radius planet camera). Old links omit them and keep their original conditions. Separate finite plate and ray-path clocks stop on exit, hidden/offscreen state and parent pause. The path clock only reveals existing illustrative ray paths: it supplies no measured arrival times, waveforms or speed solution.

The visual intention is readable terrain and connected underground structure rather than two isolated rectangular markers. Material and cut-face textures use retained coordinates and stable noise. Local orange denotes a source/transport illustration, not the physical colour of Earth's entire mantle. Established subduction remains a specified prerequisite, not something starting inevitably from two unconnected plates. The pure transform example offsets the same reference channel horizontally and introduces no new crust or melt. The new cross-section is not calibrated thickness, mountain height, stress, material volume conservation or an earthquake/eruption forecast. The true-radius global Earth retains its original radii; enlarged shallow columns remain a separate scale.

Primary sources rechecked: [USGS, Understanding plate motions](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/understanding.html) for distinct spreading, subduction, collision and transform settings; [EarthScope/IRIS, Seismic shadow zone](https://www.iris.edu/hq/inclass/animation/seismic_shadow_zone_basic_introduction) for direct S blocking and P refraction/shadows. Existing source-ledger qualifications remain in force. All added terrain, materials and channel geometry are procedural; no institutional artwork or observational imagery was copied.

Validation will record actual mesh contacts and gap-free ridge fill, no-motion/reversible geometry, strict old/new state restoration, scene-lifecycle limits and bilingual desktop/mobile browser observations. This is local draft refinement, not publication or a claim of geodynamic simulation.
