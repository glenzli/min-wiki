## Explicitly rejected inferences

- Distance 5% closer → all oceans certainly evaporate: unsupported by 1/r² alone.
- No intrinsic magnetic field → no atmosphere: contradicted by the Venus comparison and multiple escape controls.
- Mass 20% lower → rapid water loss: requires radius, thermodynamics, radiation, replenishment and history.
- No Moon → necessarily uninhabitable: not established by the cited numerical studies.
- Terminator → auroral production: different mechanisms.
- Every cold parcel sinks, or all circulation follows one conveyor: temperature, salinity, surroundings, winds and mixing matter.
