The sky has points of light and sometimes a streak that quickly disappears. These belong to astronomy, but they are not all the same. Choose a name. Is it one body, some material, a group of members or something happening?

The Sun obtains energy from within. The Moon reflects sunlight. Some clouds glow, while others block light from behind them. Both bright and dark appearances invite a question: where does the light come from? One clue alone cannot identify everything.

The Solar System has the Sun, planets and many smaller members. The Milky Way has many stellar systems. A star cluster groups stars, while a galaxy group brings galaxies together. Read the name carefully to discover which level you are exploring.

Choose a path. Follow a comet toward the Sun, compare the endings of stars, explore the effects of gravity or rotate a galaxy. Take one question into each study, and return with a new question to explore.
