Panel b is specimen USNM 139214. Every black scale bar in this plate is 1 cm. Other panels are different specimens and preservation orientations. The paper identifies these as Burgess Shale material; this plate does not establish a specimen-by-specimen Walcott Quarry provenance. The interactive scene's approximate 508 Ma Walcott context must not be silently transferred to every photographed specimen.

The 3D model is original procedural work constrained primarily by this article's Figures 20–23 and anatomical descriptions; it is not a trace, scan or animation copied from the paper or a museum. It combines anatomical information from several specimens. Colors, living thickness, surface finish, placement and motion are qualified reconstructions. The model's representative 50 mm midline body length excludes antennules, from the anterior median sclerite to the caudal-ramus tip. The paper measured 28 complete specimens, mostly about 40–60 mm; this is not a population census.

The older Royal Ontario Museum artwork and the copyrighted 2015/2016 brood-care figures are **not** bundled. No uploaded visitor photographs are used. The article's CC BY permission is specific to the included plate and does not relicense those other works.
