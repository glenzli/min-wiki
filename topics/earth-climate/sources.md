# Climate gallery source ledger

## Multiscale workbench additions — 2026-10-25

- [NSIDC · Science of Sea Ice](https://nsidc.org/learn/parts-cryosphere/sea-ice/science-sea-ice), actually opened and read formation, brine and snow sections: seawater freezing differs from snow-derived land ice; salt is largely excluded from pure ice crystals and some brine drains while some remains. P1's fixed pores, channels and exported marks are original uncalibrated structure, not a salinity or age assay.
- [NSIDC · Science of Glaciers](https://nsidc.org/learn/parts-cryosphere/glaciers/science-glaciers), actually read growth and movement sections: snow/firn compaction, changing pores and downward/outward ice movement. The valley envelope displays mass observations and does not calculate velocities; terminus retreat must not be read as uphill material flow.
- [NPS · Glacial Till and Glacial Flour](https://www.nps.gov/articles/glacialtillandglacialflour.htm) and [Glacial Striations](https://www.nps.gov/articles/glacialstriations.htm), actual specific articles opened: mixed glacial grain sizes and abrasion by rock debris carried in ice. No article photograph or display asset was copied. P3/P4 show original hypothetical observations and cannot diagnose glaciation from appearance alone.
- [NSIDC · Ice-core climate history](https://nsidc.org/learn/ask-scientist/core-climate-history), re-opened for modern core and bubble context. Existing gas/ice chronology sources below remain relevant. P5 explicitly demonstrates modern methods, not Cryogenian air recovered from an ancient ice core.
- **Original teaching geometry, not a sourced reconstruction:** on a reference sphere, two caps have fraction `f=1−sin(edge latitude)`. The authored irregular edge is `sin(edge(lon))=(1−f)+f(1−f)[0.065sin(5(lon−lonA))+0.035sin(9(lon−lonA))]`, whose longitude perturbation averages to zero and vanishes at A. Tests integrate reference-sphere area and verify A at 25/30% and the closed 100% endpoint. Surface topography, shore, ice texture, latitude guide, local section and P1–P5 structures are original code, not paleogeography, measured ice thickness or climate-model output.
- **Independent presentation clocks:** 18 global display seconds, twelve local rounds and 12 sample display seconds are not shared geological time. Global stage drives site/sea-ice context and the same chosen uniform reflection budget; it does not derive local glacier mass, sample provenance, temperature or evidence ages. Land and sea branches have different water sources.

Checked on 2026-10-25. References are exact source pages, not general institutional homepages. Source texts inform original explanations and diagrams; no institutional display art, visitor photo or long copied passage is published.

## Review resolutions

The independent science review found a genuine near-time-window label error; it was corrected to 150/120/90/60/30/0 **ka**, with a regression test. The floating-ice output now separately reports meltwater produced and direct sea-level equivalent; zero in the latter does not mean zero meltwater. Left-clipped chronological backgrounds are marked as continuations, and direct LGM/ice-core/ice-shelf sources were added. These resolutions supersede the pending items in the timestamped `SCIENCE-REVIEW.md`; that file is retained as evidence of the review.
