Two equal-sized seawater samples can have different masses and therefore different densities. Temperature and salt both matter. If colder water is also much fresher, compare both changes together.

Winds drive many surface currents. In some places, cooling and increasing salt contribute to deep-water formation. Mixing and upwelling help water return. The ocean has many branches, rather than one pipe wrapped around Earth.
