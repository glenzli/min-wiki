Now press the button to enlarge a taste bud. The tongue has many small bumps. Some contain a group of cells called a taste bud. Look at the long cells, with their tops close to a small opening. When substances contact this area, the cells can respond and pass information to nerves. Pause and find the dots. Where do the nerves connect? The whole strawberry certainly does not travel inside a nerve.

Our basic tastes include sweet, sour, salty, bitter and umami, or savory. They have not divided the tongue into five places that each do only one job.
