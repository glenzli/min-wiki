# Flight cover

`cover.svg` is an original topic-owned editorial illustration drawn in code for this contribution. It depicts a conventional passenger aircraft above softly layered clouds, with a restrained blue-gray, cream and teal palette. No external photograph, generated asset or third-party artwork was used.

The perspective, wing dimensions, clouds and light are illustrative and not measurements, a specified aircraft model or evidence of a flight trajectory. The cover carries no scientific arrows or quantitative labels; the experimental SVGs independently explain those relationships. Its 1200×800 (3:2) composition leaves space around the airplane for catalog cropping. Root integration should inspect actual catalog/search crops alongside the other modern-technology covers.
