# 火山：从岩浆到山、岛与湖 / Volcano explorer

One bilingual project at `/topics/volcano-eruption/` combines shield and composite volcanoes, an Omuroyama-inspired scoria cone, submarine volcanism and volcanic lakes. Four learning units contain seven question-led studies. Entry does not require naming a volcano: mechanism, eruption styles, retained mountain building, submarine growth, ecological conditions, lake retention, and groundwater heat. Landforms and environments are comparisons within the relevant question, not peer categories beside lake outcomes.

## Scientific scope

The construction question distinguishes a local deformation prelude from adding new volcanic rock. Plate settings and hotspots influence magma sources and ascent pathways; this scene does not simulate regional plate motion or lift the whole cone as a rigid object. An advancing intrusion and a shared, localized displacement field affect the ground and basement contacts before and during the first retained deposits. That transient field vanishes before the mature mountain and ecological histories. Inflation is exaggerated for inspection and does not guarantee a later eruption. Lava and representative ejected clasts show the source of subsequent accumulation. Their counts, sizes, trajectories and retained deposits are not a conserved-volume, pressure, fracture or collision model.

Long-term storage evolves locally as the same construction history accumulates deposits: distinct pockets widen, extend, shift or narrow at different stages of recharge and transfer. A bounded deformation maps the same closed source body and its connected feeder sheets, with material coordinates retaining crystal/grain identity. The static source isosurface is not recomputed on each frame. This is one selected teaching history, not measured reservoir growth, melt volume, pressure, fracture mechanics or a rule that every volcano follows the same storage sequence. The single-eruption question retains its separate shorter clock and source shape.

Melt occupies uneven connected pockets within that evolving framework. Spatially irregular lobes and necks replace a common horizontal fill level; recharge and transfer alter their extent. Orange-red heat windows remain distinct from darker crystal cores and contact rind, without a temperature scale or an empty underground lake. Slow local material flow and restrained, asynchronous lighting continue automatically while a region remains hot, including when process playback is paused. This phase changes neither storage volume nor deposition. Completed surface flows become dark rock at their emplacement coordinates and can be buried by later terrain.

造山问题把局部形变和新岩石堆积分开：板块环境与热点影响岩浆的来源和通道；岩浆沿裂隙侵入时，附近地面可暂时鼓起，随后熔岩和喷落碎屑留下山体。鼓胀不保证一定喷发。较长的建山时间里，补给与输送让不同储存囊体局部外扩、延伸或收窄；同一闭合岩体、相接岩脉和材质身份连续形变，内部熔体沿不均匀囊体及颈部改变分布，不按一条水平液位整齐升降。轮廓、形变、层数、代表碎屑和时间都是教学重建，不表示实测储存区、体积、压力、断裂或所有火山必经的演化。橙红热窗与暗色晶体、冷接触壳区分，颜色不能读取温度。仍热熔体的细微流动和局部明暗默认持续，不另设暂停按钮；过程暂停和拖动只改变地质进度，不让储存区自行长大。冷却的旧流仍保持落定位置，并可被新堆积覆盖。

## Eruption scenarios and relative strength

The surface-style question opens at a developed, connected episode position so terrain-bound lava is already visible. Six scene presets expose single-vent overflow, multiple discrete vents, low spatter, a high fountain, a low ash column and a stronger ash column. The paired small/large controls keep the existing mountain, process progress and camera. Gas and viscosity still determine the eruption form; strength changes relative source/product extent within that form. The fountain and ash pairs differ only in strength. Multiple vents divide the same source and do not automatically make the eruption larger. The three discrete vents do not claim a continuous surface fissure.

`eruptionScenarios.ts` owns the pure presets. `model.ts` supplies the canonical bounded strength weights; `eruptionGeometry.ts` and `canvasScene.ts` consume them for clast activity/trajectories, lava width/spread and ash height/radius/density. The 3D jet consumes the same weight. Legacy callers without a strength retain their prior default output. The labels are qualitative contrasts, not measured erupted volume, discharge, VEI classes or forecasts. The distinction between eruption size and rate follows [USGS: How big is an eruption?](https://www.usgs.gov/news/volcano-watch-how-big-eruption); compositional and eruption-style context follows [NPS: Volcanic eruptions](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/volcanoes/volcanic-eruptions.htm).

## Question navigation and legacy links

The primary menu groups seven questions under four learning units. Each question has its own retained progress, conditions and lens; changing a shape or environment preserves that question’s progress. Next-question copy explains the shift in mechanism or timescale, without implying a universal eruption-to-grass-to-lake-to-spring sequence. Existing case/chapter URLs remain valid and enter the corresponding question. Registered Markdown source links use language-qualified study anchors because the Reader rewrites registered pages while discarding arbitrary query parameters; the controller resolves these anchors and canonicalizes to `?study=...`.

The hydrothermal question illustrates a volcanic hot-rock source; not all natural hot springs require volcanism. Rainwater remains water throughout the path. Relative supply and the dry-pool threshold are uncalibrated contrasts, without temperature, flow-rate, boiling, chemistry or geothermal-power calculations. Groundwater heat can coexist with volcanic activity.

温泉默认先看地表泉池，可另选“追踪地下水”或用 `study=heat&view=section` 进入剖面。多处降水入渗沿不同深度的窄裂隙通过热岩体，在同一返回段汇合；薄水迹嵌在切面里，不构造封闭管道或地下水腔。池岸是不对称浅台地与矿物岸坡，固定泉源的薄水膜贴着岩床流入泉池，低幅涟漪、池内流动与薄雾只在热水出露时出现。蓝到暖色只是受热编码，不是水的真实颜色、温度刻度或沸腾。薄雾表示较凉空气中冷凝的小水滴；真实可见度还受环境影响。封堵回路或去掉水补给会去掉对应池面活动。固定资源和已有显示相位保持原有隐藏、减少动态、释放规则。参考 [USGS 热泉与间歇泉](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-relationship-between-volcanism-and-geysers-and-hot-springs-yellowstone)；本例不把普通温泉当作沸腾喷泉。

Hot springs open at the surface pool; “Follow groundwater” or `study=heat&view=section` opens the cutaway. Separate recharge sites follow narrow fractures at different depths through the hot rock and merge into one return. Thin water traces sit in the exposed face, without constructing a closed pipe or underground water cavity. An asymmetric shelf and mineral banks surround the pool; a fixed seep sends a thin water sheet along the actual rock bed into the pool. Small ripples, pool circulation and light condensation appear only with warm discharge. Blue-to-warm colour is a heating code, not water's actual colour, a temperature scale or boiling. Mist denotes droplets condensed in cooler air; actual visibility also depends on atmospheric conditions. Blocking the return or removing recharge removes the corresponding pool activity. Fixed resources and the existing detail phase retain hidden/reduced-motion/disposal rules. [USGS hot springs and geysers](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-relationship-between-volcanism-and-geysers-and-hot-springs-yellowstone) constrains the distinction; ordinary hot springs are not represented as boiling fountains.

海底问题把海洋情境与“看海底生命／看成岛后的生命”放在画面下方，另选适合定居的后续条件并靠近冷岩；“回到刚才的喷发”恢复进入前的进度、镜头与裸岩/定居条件。保留默认裸岩对照，不把生物出现当作每次喷发的终点；其独立责任与科学限定见 [海底专题说明](../submarine-volcanoes/README.md)。火山湖剖面中的局部失撑间隙、断口凹深和残余晶粥由 [湖盆专题](../volcanic-lakes/README.md)拥有；间隙放大用于辨认，随后由同一岩体下沉闭合，不表示一个充满空气的巨大地下洞穴。

The submarine question exposes its ocean setting and “Explore seafloor life / Explore life on the new island” directly below the scene. The entry selects suitable later conditions and a cooled-rock close view; “Return to the eruption” restores the prior progress, lens and bare/suitable setting. The default bare-rock contrast remains, without making colonisation the endpoint of every eruption; ownership and qualifications are in the [submarine topic](../submarine-volcanoes/README.md). The [lake topic](../volcanic-lakes/README.md) owns local loss-of-support gaps, recessed fracture faces and residual mush. Enlarged gaps close as the same rock descends; they do not depict a giant air-filled underground cavern.
