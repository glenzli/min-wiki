01 · A small message begins
[Begin with the whole diagram, zoom into the skin endings, then return to the full routes.]

Have you ever touched something and quickly pulled your hand away? Today we will not touch anything hot or hurt ourselves. We will follow a little message on the screen instead. First, zoom into the skin. Fine nerve endings there can respond to some changes that might harm the body. What they send is a nerve signal, not a tiny ball of pain traveling through you.

02 · A place where routes branch
[Advance to the spinal branch and pause. Emphasize each route while retaining both.]

Now let the picture move slowly. Follow the warm-colored dot along the nerve to a place called the spinal cord. Pause and find the two routes that branch here. The green one passes through a circuit in the body and connects with a motor nerve and muscle. The purple one carries information toward the brain. You can emphasize each route in turn. A dimmer route is still working; it has not really switched off.

---

03 · The body can protect itself first
[Advance to withdrawal and pause to compare the muscle with the continuing ascending signal.]

Keep watching. The green route reaches the muscle, and the illustrated hand pulls back a little. This protective action is called a reflex. Sometimes the body starts moving before we have had time to decide what to do. Now look at the purple route: information is still traveling onward. Pulling away and clearly feeling pain are related, but they are not exactly the same event. They are not one single button.

---

04 · Listen carefully to the body
[Finish on multiple brain-processing nodes, the full routes and the care message.]

In the brain, information is processed along with the body’s condition, what we notice and our past experiences. Pain can tell us that we need protection and care, but it is not a ruler for measuring a wound. A small-looking wound can hurt a lot. No visible wound does not mean pain is pretend. If you feel unwell, tell an adult who cares for you. Together, you can listen carefully to what your body is telling you.
